## 4. General FAQs

**Q1. What is Gmail (Web version)?**  

**A:** Gmail (Web version) is the browser-based version of Gmail developed by Google that allows users to send, receive, and organize emails using a web browser.

---

**Q2. Which operating systems are supported?**  

**A:** Gmail can be accessed on any operating system with a modern browser, including Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, and ChromeOS.

---

## 5. Account & Login FAQs

**Q1. How do I log into Gmail?**  

**A:**

1. Open Gmail in a browser  
2. Enter your email address → click **Next**  
3. Enter your password → click **Next**  
4. Click **Sign in**

---

**Q2. Why can't I log in?**  

**A:** Ensure you have a stable internet connection and that your credentials are correct.

---

## 6. Email Sending & Receiving FAQs

**Q1. How do I send an email?**  

**A:**

1. Click **Compose**  
2. Enter recipient email in **To**  
3. Enter subject and message  
4. Click **Send**

---

**Q2. How do I view received emails?**  

**A:** Emails appear in the **Inbox** by default.

---

## 7. Features FAQs

**Q1. How do I change maximum page size?**  

**A:**

1. Click **Settings**  
2. Select **See all settings**  
3. Under **General**, find **Maximum page size**  
4. Choose number of conversations  
5. Click **Save Changes**

---

**Q2. How do I archive an email?**  

**A:**

1. Select the email  
2. Click **Archive icon** or right-click → **Archive**

---

## 8. Troubleshooting FAQs

**Q1. Why does my email fail to send?**  

**A:** This may happen due to incorrect email address or unstable internet connection. Verify details and retry.

---

**Q2. Why am I not receiving emails?**  

**A:** Storage may be full. Delete unwanted emails and clear the **Trash** folder.

---

## 9. Security & Privacy FAQs

**Q1. Is Gmail data encrypted?**  

**A:** Yes, Gmail uses HTTPS (TLS) encryption in transit and encryption at rest.

---

**Q2. How is personal data protected?**  

**A:** Gmail uses encryption, spam filtering, phishing protection, and advanced security systems.

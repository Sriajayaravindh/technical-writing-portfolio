## 4. General FAQs

**Q1. What is Google Chrome?**  

**A:** Google Chrome is a free web browser developed by Google that allows users to access websites on the internet.

---

**Q2. Which operating systems are supported?**  

**A:** Supported operating systems:

**Mobile:**
- Android (8.0 or later)  
- iOS (16 or later)  

**Desktop:**
- Windows (10 or later)  
- macOS (Big Sur or later)  
- Linux (64-bit, glibc 2.17 or later)

## 5. Installation FAQs

**Q1. How do I install Google Chrome on Windows?**  

**A:**

1. Go to the official Chrome website  
2. Click **Download Chrome**  
3. Run the installer file  

---

**Q2. Why does installation fail?**  

**A:** It may fail due to low disk space, a corrupted installer, or an outdated OS.

## 6. Account & Login FAQs

**Q1. How do I log in to Chrome?**  

**A:**

1. Click **Profile icon** (top right)  
2. Click **Sign in to Chrome**  
3. Enter email → **Next**  
4. Enter password → **Next**

---

**Q2. Why am I unable to log in?**  

**A:** Check your internet connection and verify your login credentials.

## 7. Feature FAQs

**Q1. How do I set Chrome as default browser?**  

**A:**

1. Open **Chrome → Settings → Default browser**  
2. Click **Make Default**

---

**Q2. How do I clear cache?**  

**A:**

**Desktop:**

1. Open **Chrome → Settings**  
2. Go to **Privacy and security → Clear browsing data**  
3. Select **Cached images and files**  
4. Click **Clear data**

**Mobile (Android):**

1. Open **Chrome**  
2. Tap **⋮ → Settings**  
3. Tap **Privacy and security → Clear browsing data**  
4. Select cache → Tap **Clear data**

**Mobile (iOS):**

1. Open **Chrome**  
2. Tap **⋮ → Settings**  
3. Tap **Privacy → Clear Browsing Data**  
4. Select cache → Confirm

---

## 8. Troubleshooting FAQs

**Q1. Chrome fails to update. Why?**  

**A:** This happens due to low disk space or poor internet. Free space and retry.

---

**Q2. Why is Chrome slow?**  

**A:** Causes include:

- Too many tabs  
- Outdated version  
- Low system memory  

**Fix:**
- Close unused tabs  
- Clear cache  
- Update via **Settings → Help → About Chrome**

---

## 9. Security & Privacy FAQs

**Q1. Is user data encrypted?**  

**A:** Yes, Chrome uses encryption, sandboxing, Safe Browsing, and updates to protect data.

---

**Q2. How is personal information protected?**  

**A:** Through encryption, sandboxing, Safe Browsing, auto-updates, and 2-step verification.
